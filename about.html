ABOUT GURU COMMUNICATION

More than a repair counter: We operate an industrial-grade mobile technology laboratory, machinery distribution center, and precision display refurbishing cleanroom.

Pioneering High-Precision Mobile Engineering

Founded over a decade ago with a mission to eliminate disposable electronics culture, **Guru Communication** brings factory-grade diagnostics and chip-level restoration directly to end consumers and commercial repair businesses.

While conventional repair shops swap whole modular assemblies at high costs, our cleanroom facility specializes in microscopic component restoration: micro-soldering damaged logic board traces, reprogramming Face ID dot projectors, and utilizing YMT vacuum OCA laminators to replace cracked outer glass while preserving 100% of your factory-original OLED display panel.

Dust-Free Cleanroom Stations

Class 100 positive-pressure laminar flow workstations ensure zero dust contamination during optical OCA display lamination.

10+ Years of Proven Expertise

Over 5,000 successful complex repairs completed across Apple iOS, Samsung Galaxy, and all flagship Android platforms.

Authorized YMT Equipment Partner

Direct importer and distributor of authentic YMT vacuum laminators, laser separators, and commercial repair tooling.

25-Point Rigorous Quality Control

Every device undergoes comprehensive post-repair testing: touch digitizer latency, proximity sensor, cellular antennas, thermals, and battery health.
